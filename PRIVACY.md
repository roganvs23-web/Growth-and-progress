# Momentum privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Momentum is a habit coach. This page explains what it reads, what leaves your phone, and how to delete it. Questions: **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## The short version
- Your plan, history and settings are stored **only on your phone**. Momentum has no accounts.
- Momentum reads **daily totals** (not minute-by-minute data) from Apple Health or Health Connect, and only the ones you allow.
- If you turn on the AI coach, your goal, plan, chat and those daily totals are sent to the Momentum server and then to Anthropic's Claude to write replies. Nothing is sold, used for ads, or used to train AI.
- You can export or delete everything at any time in **You**.

## What Momentum reads
With your permission, from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android): sleep, steps, workout minutes, resting heart rate and heart rate variability. You can switch each one off in **You > Privacy**, and change access in your phone's settings. Momentum never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

You may also enter: your year of birth (only the year), whether you are a student, a morning check-in (sleep and how you feel), habit ticks, counts and slip-up triggers, and messages to the coach.

## What leaves your phone
| Feature | What is sent | To whom | Kept? |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI coach (optional, you are asked first) | Goal, plan, chat, weekly habit stats and up to 7 days of daily health totals, and an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or adult) | The Momentum server, which passes it to Anthropic (Claude) to write a reply | The Momentum server does not store it. Anthropic does not use it to train models and keeps API data only for a limited time under its [commercial terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms). |
| Buddy mode (optional) | A nickname you choose, your current streak, whether today is a good day yet, and cheers | The Momentum server, shown only to the one friend who entered your code | Until either of you leaves buddy mode or deletes all data |
| Nothing else | | | |

The Momentum server uses your IP address only to limit how many requests one device can make, and does not save it. The hosting provider may keep standard access logs for a short time.

## What Momentum never does
- Sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
- Use health data for advertising or marketing.
- Store health data in iCloud or on our server.

## Children and teens
Momentum asks for your year of birth. Under 18s get a coach that never gives diet or weight-loss advice. [UNDER-13 POLICY: to be decided before launch.]

## Your choices
- **Export:** You > Your data > Export my data.
- **Delete:** You > Your data > Delete all my data. This erases everything on the phone and removes your buddy link from the server. Uninstalling the app also erases the data on the phone.
- **Stop sharing:** switch off the AI coach, a metric, or buddy mode at any time.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
